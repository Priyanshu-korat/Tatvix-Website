## Evidence policy

Company service scope and technology options were informed by the existing public Tatvix services page and user-supplied source repository `https://github.com/IndiGtech/TatvixTech`. Four case-study records retain their published content and dates. New industry pages describe considerations and potential scope; they are not new portfolio claims. The LinkedIn identity comes from the existing company site.

Do not publish the screenshot's “4+ years”, “50+ projects”, “15+ industries”, “99% on-time”, named testimonials or client logos solely because they appeared in a screenshot. Establish the source, definition, current accuracy and publication permission first. If verified later, use a small readable evidence section linked to relevant work; a rotating logo/testimonial carousel is optional and must remain accessible.

## Ongoing improvement

Use actual Search Console/Bing query and enquiry data after launch to choose useful additions. Prioritise original case studies, clear technical explanations and customer questions supported by company expertise. Record authorship, real publication/update dates and source evidence. Avoid mass-generated sector/location pages, hidden keywords, fake reviews, purchased links and unsupported certification claims.
